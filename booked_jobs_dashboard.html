Sources. Booked jobs = count of rows in Booking PBI 4DX.xlsx (Sheet1) grouped by Created Date — every row with a Created Date counts as one booked job (17,425 dated rows; 262 rows had no Created Date and are excluded). Goal = Booked Jobs Goal column on the Daily Goals tab of CBD 2026 Revenue Budget.xlsx. Both files on SharePoint (comfortbydesign.com).
Yesterday = the prior calendar day (Aug 11, 2026). Last-year comparison uses a 364-day shift so the weekday matches (Tue → Tue).
Goal coverage. The Booked Jobs Goal column is populated on business days only and currently runs Jan 2 – Aug 31, 2026. Weekends, holidays and unbudgeted months carry no goal and are omitted from goal sums and markers rather than treated as zero. Period goals are summed through the last Created Date in the data (Aug 11, 2026), not through today. Weeks start Monday. Attainment: ≥100% green, 95–99.9% amber, <95% red. YoY: ≥0 green, <0 red.

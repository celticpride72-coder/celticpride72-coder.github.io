Potential Actions to Improve GP%

🤖 RESEARCHED WITH AI · AUG 2026 HVAC & hearth industry benchmarks, mapped to CBD's numbers above. Directional guidance — validate against your own P&L before acting.
1 · Grow maintenance / membership agreements
Maintenance work runs 55–65% GP vs 35–45% on transactional repair. Shops with 30%+ of revenue from memberships beat emergency-only operations by 4–6 net margin points, with 80%+ retention and higher follow-on sales.
For CBD: Service ran negative GP in July and is ~$76K under budget YTD — shifting the service mix toward agreements is the highest-margin way to pull it back into the black and smooth shoulder seasons.
2 · Raise technician billable efficiency & utilization
Most shops run 55–65% billable efficiency; target 70–80%. Lifting 60%→75% on ~6 techs can add ~$500K revenue with no new headcount; a 10% efficiency gain lifts net profit several points.
For CBD: service wages are ~70%+ of service revenue — better dispatch/routing, enforced job closeout, and right-sizing labor to demand is the fastest lever on the negative Service GP.
3 · Move to option-based (Good–Better–Best) pricing
Presenting tiered options with IAQ/accessory add-ons moves average ticket $1,800–$3,200 and lifts attach rates (IAQ 8%→34% in top shops); premium tiers carry the highest GP.
For CBD: applies to both service and install — a higher ticket at a higher margin without adding trucks or leads.
4 · Tighten material/equipment markup & estimating
Holding 50–55% GP typically requires materials at 2.5–3.5× wholesale and equipment near 300–400% markup, with actual job costs fed back into estimates.
For CBD: Fireplace-Install materials run ~47% of revenue (vs ~21% HVAC) and its GP has slipped below budget and prior year — a markup/estimating review on surrounds & parts is the direct fix.
5 · Negotiate procurement — volume, terms, rebates
As volume grows, negotiate better pricing and rebates on equipment, copper/steel, and hearth surrounds/parts; bulk agreements let you hold margin even on competitive bids.
For CBD: the material-heavy Fireplace installs benefit most — vendor rebates and bulk terms drop straight to GP.
6 · Cut callbacks / raise first-time-fix rate
Target 80%+ first-time-fix; callbacks above ~2% of weekly revenue are a margin leak — every repeat visit is a truck roll with labor cost and zero revenue.
For CBD: the Callbacks dashboard shows ~13% of jobs needing 2+ visits — each avoided repeat is pure GP recovered.
7 · Protect installation-labor pricing (hearth)
Hearth retailers make their profit on installation labor, parts, accessories and service — the norm is to discount the unit but not the install; hearth service/maintenance runs 60–70% GP.
For CBD: guard Fireplace-install labor pricing and grow attached service, parts & inspections rather than competing on install price.
8 · Job-costing discipline — price off fully-burdened cost
Start every price from fully-loaded labor (wage + burden + truck + overhead/hour) plus true material cost (freight + inventory carry), and enforce job closeout before invoicing.
For CBD: the departments missing budget GP (Service, Fireplace Install) are exactly where actual-vs-estimate feedback catches the leakage.
Sources: ServiceTitan, Simpro, FieldEdge, Pipeline On, ApplauseHQ, Striven (hearth), Contracting Business, CEO Finance Academy, Profitability Partners — HVAC & hearth profit-margin and operations research, 2025–2026. AI-synthesized; benchmarks are industry ranges, not CBD-specific promises.
